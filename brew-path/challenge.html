BREWPATH · BREW CHALLENGE WALKTHROUGH

One brew challenge, start to stamp.

A Brew Challenge is a small, optional real-life coffee task tied to what you just learned — “taste two origins side by side,” “time your bloom.” This walkthrough follows a single challenge through its whole life: it’s offered when a module wraps, you accept it, it waits on Today, you log how it went, and it leaves a permanent stamp on the collectible card and a filled node on your Path. Tap inside any frame to use it.
