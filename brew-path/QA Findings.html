What the machine cannot check

Everything below this section is structure. None of it tells you whether the course is any good — that needs you, and it is the more valuable pass.

noteIs each question worth askingjudgement
A card can be structurally perfect and still test a detail that does not matter, or give its answer away through phrasing. Watch for questions where the correct option is simply the longest or most specific — the commonest tell in authored quizzes.
noteDo the explanations teachjudgement
Every graded card has an explanation, but I can only confirm one exists. Whether it explains why the wrong answers are wrong is the difference between a quiz and a lesson.
notePacingjudgement
257 cards across 32 lessons averages about 8 per lesson. Whether a particular lesson drags, or stops before it lands, only shows up when you play it.
noteReal-device feeljudgement
Safe areas, thumb reach, hairline visibility at low brightness, and whether the drill timers feel generous or stressful. None of it is visible in a desktop preview.
