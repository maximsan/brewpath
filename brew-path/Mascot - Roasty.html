Meet Roasty.

A tiny coffee bean with two stubborn green leaves and a quiet curiosity for the world. Roasty stays at your side while you learn — celebrating small wins, softening small misses, and growing as you do.

Roasty,
/ˈroʊ-stee/ · noun

noun — the slow, careful change a green seed makes when it meets heat. In coffee, it's the moment a bean stops being a seed and starts being a cup. Roasty is exactly that: a quiet transformation.

Not a teacher. Not a mascot in the sales sense. A little companion who sits on the page beside you, blinks when you blink, and grows leaves as your coffee tree does.

warm curious soft-spoken earnest slightly sleepy grows with you
§ 01

The anatomy of a sprout.

Six parts, each tuned to be easy to animate and friendly to read at thumbnail size — even at 32 pixels Roasty should still register.

01SPROUT
Two leaves; grows in stages as the user's coffee tree progresses.
02EYES
Slightly oversized; squint when happy, soften when wrong. Never blink in sync.
03BEAN CREASE
A soft S-curve; reads as "coffee bean" even without color.
04MOUTH
Single-stroke; five total shapes carry every emotion.
05BODY
Asymmetric oval — never a circle. Slight tilt gives personality.
06CHEEKS
Subtle blush; lifts the face from "graphic" to "alive".
§ 02

The waking sequence.

Six beats, ≈3 seconds. The app opens to a sleepy bean. A drop of water lands. Two leaves push out. Roasty blinks awake, smiles, waves you in.

§ 04

Motion vocabulary.

A small, opinionated set. Every animation is one or a blend of these primitives — keeps Roasty from feeling jittery or over-acted.

Breath
Always on. 3.2s loop, vertical bob, ±3px.
EASE · in-out
USED IN · idle, all
Sway
Leaves only. 2.8s loop, ±2° from anchor.
EASE · in-out
USED IN · idle, happy
Hop
Single bounce. 900ms, springy on the way up.
EASE · spring(.4,1.6)
USED IN · correct, lesson
Wobble
Side-to-side shake. 500ms, decays quickly.
EASE · in-out
USED IN · wrong
