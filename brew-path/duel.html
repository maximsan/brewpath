BREWPATH · COFFEE DUEL

Challenge a friend, async.

One player runs a short 5-question coffee challenge and sends the link to a friend; the friend plays the same five, then both see the head-to-head. No matchmaking, no real-time, no leaderboards — just lightweight sharing and friendly competition. Every required state is a live frame below. Tap inside any to play it.
