BREWPATH · LESSON WALKTHROUGH

One lesson, card by card.

A lesson is a short stack of cards you swipe through in a few minutes. It opens with a framing card, teaches a couple of concepts, then checks understanding with a run of interactive cards — multiple choice, match, calibrate, order — before landing a single takeaway and paying out the reward. This walkthrough follows the “Arabica vs Robusta” lesson through every card kind. Tap inside any frame to play it.
