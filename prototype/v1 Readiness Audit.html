AMENDMENT · AUG 2026 · SUPERSEDES “THE V1 MODEL” BELOW

Monetization pivoted from subscription to one-time purchase. The Plus subscription (monthly/yearly, 7-day trial) is dropped. v1 sells one product: Foundations, a one-time purchase at $19.99 with permanent access — no trial, nothing renews. The purchase contains Modules 2–5 (with their Brew Challenges and cards), every practice format, the complete Dictionary, unlimited Saved, and the Studio (Roasty + grove customization). Free keeps all of Module 1 — its lessons, challenges and module test — plus Flashcards and Guess the Term (both streak-eligible), short entries for lesson-earned dictionary terms, a Saved shelf of 5, and all progress systems on accessible content. (Amended again 18 Aug: the free taster widened from the first two lessons to the whole of Module 1. Amended 19 Aug: True or false and Match the facts join the free tier as the free mini-game pair, per the DS access-tier table; the five palate/ordering formats remain in Foundations. Monetization experiments planned — one-time purchase stays the shipping baseline; a subscription-only arm and a hybrid arm (subscribe or buy outright, the primary test candidate) are now switchable in the prototype via Tweaks → Purchases → Model. Paywall presentation is config-driven (monetization.jsx); entitlement logic is model-agnostic by contract.) Future substantial courses (Advanced Brewing, Espresso, Coffee Origins) sell separately as one-time purchases; the Courses catalogue stays hidden until a second course exists. Fixes and improvements to a purchased course remain included. The subscription copy in the Model section below stands as the historical record of the decision this amendment replaces.

Verdict

Strong build, oversized for a first release.

The learning loop, the visual language, and the component library are genuinely v1-grade. The risk is shipping five products at once — a course, a dictionary, an atlas, an async duel, and a cosmetics store — each pulling content, infra and QA a different way.

Decision
Ship the learning core + dictionary. Hold Atlas, Duel and ads for v2.

Keeps everything that delivers “learn coffee, a little every day,” and drops the two features that need a backend you don’t need yet plus a large second content vertical.

Subscription only — and streak stays free

Two prices, one product. A Lifetime tier was considered and dropped: a non-renewing plan needs its own receipt, restore and manage-plan states. Streak protection was also cut from the bundle — the streak carries no in-app reward, so selling insurance on it is selling insurance on an item with no value, and repair pitches at the exact moment a user has already lapsed. Streak freeze stays free — and is now earned (one per 7 days, two held, spent automatically) rather than switched on in Settings, so it is felt as a reward instead of hidden as a preference. Cosmetic IAPs and a weekly-goal setting both defer to v2.

Why defer, not delete

Atlas and Duel are the obvious v2 marketing beats — “explore the coffee belt,” “challenge a friend.” Holding them gives v2 a story and v1 a fighting chance at quality. The work is waiting, not wasted.

The shape of v1

Four tabs, one clear loop

With Atlas and Duel held back, the tab bar drops from five to four and the app tightens around the thing it does best.

TabsToday · Path · Cards · Profile. Atlas leaves the tab bar for v2. Saved and the Dictionary stay as the pinned top-right entries; the Duel entry goes with it.
LoopOpen → today’s lesson → play the cards → earn points + a collectible → streak ticks → tree grows. Dictionary is one tap away. A complete daily product.
Result~40% less surface to QA, two fewer infra dependencies, one monetization model, and a v2 roadmap with two finished headline features waiting.
What’s left

Two engineering tasks, and one that is yours.

Three tasks, in the order they block a release.

Wire StoreKit
Receipt validation and restore, and swap the frozen trial counter for a real one. The only paid-model work left, and it is engineering — the design side is done.
Gate the dev Tweaks panel out of production
A build-time dev conditional so the panel is absent from the store binary. Its six knobs — Scope, Progress, Restore result, Plus unlocked and the two freeze states — stay useful for QA until then. Scope must ship reading v1 cut: Everything renders the deferred surfaces for review. Restore result stands in for a store that does not exist yet and retires with StoreKit. Every key is documented at the head of app.jsx, marked panel, routed or settled; nothing reads a value that has no entry there. One line, owned by whoever wires the release build.
Play the course
The machine pass is closed; the judgement pass is not. Whether the explanations teach, whether the pacing holds, and how it reads on a real device — see the open half of QA Findings.
